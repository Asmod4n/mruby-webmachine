AF_UNIX, htgen as the client, 3 runs of 5 s for each step.

2026-10-13-220228Z-connections-constrained.md.
Machine:  Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.10GHz, 4 vCPU, L2 2 MiB per core,
kernel 6.18.44-fc-v114. This is another host than the runs of 2026-10-13 and
the earlier runs of 2026-10-14 (Xeon @ 2.80GHz). Their numbers are not
compared with these.
Container: podman, seccomp=unconfined, nofile 20000, memlock 8192 KiB.

evenly across them. cli% is the sum over them.
